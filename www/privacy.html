Privacy Policy

AN2U Books by Annamalai Shanmuganathan is a reading app for the author's own Tamil and English books. Last updated: September 2026.

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account or sign in. We do not run advertising or sell any data.

  • Contact form: if you use the Contact page, the name, email and message you type are sent to the author through the Formspree service so he can reply.
  • View counts: the app adds one to an anonymous counter for the site and for each book you open. No personal details are stored with it.
  • Natural voice (Listen): when you choose the natural voice, the sentence being read (text from the book itself) is sent to our server to make the audio. No personal details are involved.

Stored on your device only

Your reading position, bookmarks, highlights, theme, language and text settings are saved on your own phone. They are not sent to us and are removed if you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Children

The app is a general reading app and does not knowingly collect information from children.
