## Full description – English
AN2U Books is the reading app for the books of author Annamalai Shanmuganathan, in Tamil and English.

Read on screen, save paper, love nature. The app was made to encourage the habit of reading and to protect nature.

WHAT YOU GET
• Novels and short stories in Tamil and English: eco sci-fi, love stories, travel fiction, realistic short stories, and stories where everyday objects have feelings
• A clean book reader with page turning, contents, text size and page colour
• Highlight sentences, add bookmarks, and pick up where you stopped
• Listen to the book read aloud in Tamil or English
• Light and dark themes
• Switch the whole app between தமிழ் and English at any time
• No account, no login, no ads

"The magic that makes the past live in the present is Writing."
